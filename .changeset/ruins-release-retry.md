Add Ruins, a light sepia-and-graphite theme in the Supporter-only Special themes in Appearance. It brings square corners, Zen Kaku Gothic New with Share Tech Mono for metadata, ruled numbered section headers, a hard-shadow selection style and slightly sepia covers.

Ruins has its own options on each device: a boot terminal when GameNode opens (always, once a day or never; skipped by tap, click, Esc or Enter, and reduced to a short static logo when the system asks for reduced motion), an optional paper texture and a loading scanline. The app's color scheme now follows the active theme, so Mantine and Ionic components render in light colors under Ruins and stay dark under GameNode and Xbox. On Android, the launch splash follows the theme too: Ruins opens with its ink-on-paper colors, and the boot terminal starts once the splash is gone. Dark cards over game art (trending games, trending reviews, the Explore featured game) now follow the theme instead of staying black, and the release calendar's today marker stays readable when the day has no releases.
