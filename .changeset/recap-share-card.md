Redesign the Recap page around a share card: choose a period (week, month or year) and a format, Story with the Poster, Dash or Bento template or Feed with the summary, with total time played, top game, genre and platform, the top five games and the change against the previous period. Every template is built from the same period summary. The cards can be customized: choose the game whose art fills the backdrop and how visible it is, the accent color and the extra figure (finished, reviews or started), and opt in to the comparison with the previous period, which is off by default.
