Add library and per-game visibility controls, with public, followers-only and private levels. Owners see a visibility control next to their own games and collection entries, a badge on their own cards, and a "Library and collections" privacy setting; other viewers get a clear hidden-library state instead of an empty library, and the same hidden state replaces errors for entries, collections, journals and recaps they cannot see. Session-aware reads now cache per viewer so one viewer's filtered results never leak into another's.
