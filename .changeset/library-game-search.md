Search games inside a library or collection by title or alternative name. The search keeps its place in the URL across tabs, sorting and filters, and shows a clear message with a way back when nothing matches.

On phones, the library no longer shows a collection picker above the games; collections stay one tap away in the navigation. On tablets, the collections sidebar now sits beside the games instead of stacking above them.
